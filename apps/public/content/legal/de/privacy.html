Who we are

Inmolink (operated by Realty Soft) provides a multi-agent property marketplace and collaboration platform for real-estate agencies in the European Union. The data controller is Realty Soft S.L., contactable at privacy@inmolink.eu.

What we collect

  • Account data — email, password (hashed with Argon2id), name, phone, preferred locale, optional photo. Provided by you when you sign up or accept an agency invitation.
  • Property data — listing details that agency members publish to the platform; includes location, photos, price, and translatable text.
  • Lead data — name, email, phone (optional), and message you submit via the contact form on a property page. We salt and hash your IP address; the raw IP is never persisted.
  • Cookies — strictly necessary cookies (session, CSRF) and an opt-in cookie consent record. We do not use marketing cookies. See the cookie policy for details.
  • Usage logs — request method/path, status code, response time, anonymised IP. Used for monitoring and abuse prevention; PII is redacted at the log layer.

Why we process it

  • To provide the service (legal basis: performance of contract — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To send transactional emails — viewing requests, deal confirmations, ticket replies, password resets — using your agency's SMTP server when configured (legal basis: performance of contract).
  • To send marketing emails on behalf of an agency, only when the recipient has given explicit consent (legal basis: consent — Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • For platform safety, abuse prevention, and audit trails (legal basis: legitimate interests — Art. 6(1)(f)).

Where data lives

Application data resides in EU-hosted PostgreSQL. Object storage (property images, export files, ticket attachments) lives in Cloudflare R2 (EU jurisdiction). Search indexes live in Meilisearch on the same VPS. Backups are encrypted and stored in R2 with 30-day versioning.

Sub-processors

  • Cloudflare (CDN, R2 object storage)
  • Stripe (payment processing — when you upgrade to PRO)
  • Resend (transactional email — when no per-agency SMTP configured)
  • Better Stack (uptime monitoring + log retention)

Your rights (GDPR Articles 15–22)

You may request access, rectification, erasure, portability, or restriction at privacy@inmolink.eu. We respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Retention

  • Account data: while your account is active, then 90 days post-closure.
  • Property data: until you delete it (soft-delete with 30-day grace, then permanent).
  • Audit log: 24 months for security-sensitive events.
  • Backups: 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We'll publish material changes here at least 30 days before they take effect and notify active accounts by email.
